# Crowfield v0.5 plan (approved by Caleb, 2026-10-22)

Three parts: harder crows by day, more crops, and a dusk look. Every number is a first guess. Change any of it.

## 1. Crows by day

Four crow levels. Each day picks a random total in its range, then a random mix, so no two runs match.
After Day 40, every crow is gold.

| Level | Color | HP | Speed | Bites every | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lv.1 | black | 1 | today's speed | 2.0 s | |
| Lv.2 | red | 2 | +15% | 2.0 s | |
| Lv.3 | purple (new) | 3 | +30% | 1.5 s | |
| Gold | gold (new) | 5 (v0.5.1; was 4) | +45% | 1.0 s | drops 5 coins when killed |

**Eating (Caleb's call):** a crow turns purple (Lv.3) after it has eaten its 3rd whole crop. Bites no
longer add HP or tint a crow, so a crow's color always tells its level. Gold crows stay gold.

## 2. More crops

Corn drops to 2 coins (your call). Three new crops, each unlocked on a set day in the SEEDS page:

| Crop | Seed | Sells | Grows | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Corn | 1 | 2 | 24 s | Day 1 |
| Carrot | 2 | 5 | 32 s | Day 5 |
| Tomato | 3 | 8 | 40 s | Day 12 |
| Pumpkin | 5 | 14 | 56 s | Day 20 |

Better crops earn more per second but sit in the field longer, so crows have more time to eat them.

**Decisions (Caleb):**
- **L cycles which seed you plant** (unlocked seeds only). The HUD seed count shows the chosen seed.
- **Scarecrows fire corn seeds only.** With no corn they stop.
- Each crop gets its own grown and ripe look (sprout and young plant are shared).

## 3. Dusk look

**Caleb's pick: A with fireflies at night.** During the 15 s "DUSK IN n" countdown the field slowly warms to
sunset orange. When the shop opens it is night: the field dims to blue, and fireflies drift and blink
slowly around the shop window. Fireflies show only at night in the shop. The next day starts bright.
