# Crowfield v0.6 plan: the blood moon (approved by Caleb, 2026-10-22)

Caleb's idea (2026-10-22): from Day 35 the days feel long and samey. Some nights become a **blood moon**:
instead of the dusk shop, the farmer fights a ruthless night of gold crows until dawn, for a lot of coins.
Dawn opens the shop, then the next day goes on. Every number below is a first guess. Change any of it.

## When
- **The first blood moon is the night after Day 34** (so it leads into Day 35), as in Caleb's example.
- **Then every 4 to 6 nights, at random** (Caleb): after one blood moon, the next comes 4, 5, or 6 nights later.
- A warning comes the day before it, at dusk: "A BLOOD MOON RISES TOMORROW", and on the blood-moon day the
  15 s countdown reads "BLOOD MOON IN n" and the sky fades to deep red instead of orange.

## The night
1. Day 34's crows are beaten, "DAY 34 DONE!", the 15 s countdown (red sky).
2. No shop. The field turns blood-red and dark. Banner: "BLOOD MOON!".
3. Gold crows pour in, one about every second, from all edges, for **90 seconds**.
   At most 24 are on screen; the rest wait for a free slot.
4. A countdown shows "DAWN IN n". When it reaches 0 the sky turns pink, every crow still alive flies away,
   and "DAWN!" shows.
5. Dawn: the shop opens (with a dawn bonus already paid). Then Day 35 starts as usual.

## What makes it ruthless (Caleb's list)
- **All crows are gold.** They are also 20% faster than daytime gold crows.
- **You get up slower:** a knockout lasts 6 s instead of 3.
- **Scarecrows drop to Lv.1 for the night only** (Caleb): they fire at Lv.1 strength until dawn, then return
  to the level you paid for.
- **You can fail it** (Caleb): the 3rd knockout during a blood moon loses the night. After each knockout a
  message counts them: "KNOCKOUT 1 OF 3".
  - Blood moons 1-3 are practice (Caleb, "softer"): losing one takes back that night's coins and the dawn
    bonus ("LOST! NO COINS TONIGHT"); the run goes on.
  - After the 3rd, a notice shows the next morning: "NOW IS THE TIME FOR YOUR TRAINING TO SHOW. LOSE A
    BLOOD MOON NOW AND THE RUN IS OVER." From then on a lost blood moon ends the run ("THE BLOOD MOON WON").
- Crops do not grow during the night. Crows still eat them.
- The normal game-over rule still applies (no crops, no seeds, no coins).

## The reward
- Each gold crow killed during the night drops **10 coins** (double the daytime 5).
- Surviving until dawn pays a **dawn bonus of 25 coins**, shown in the shop ("BLOOD MOON BONUS +25").
- With a good defense that is roughly 150-300 coins a night, enough for a couple of upgrades.

## Looks
- Red sunset during the countdown before a blood moon, a dark blood-red field during the night, a pink dawn.
- No new art needed except a red "BLOOD MOON!" banner (text) and the existing gold crow.
