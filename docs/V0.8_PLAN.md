## 1. Blood moon crows: exact counts, in spurts (Caleb)

- The most crows a normal day brings before the first blood moon is **23** (Day 34 rolls 19-23). So
  blood moon 1 starts just above that, and the counts grow gradually.
- The crows come in **spurts that fill the whole night**: the night is split into equal parts (6 in a 30 s
  night, 9 in a 45 s final night). Each part opens with a burst (one crow every 1/4 s), then a short quiet.
  Later spurts are a little bigger. Dawn comes only when the timer runs out.
- Only 24 crows fit on screen; the rest wait for a free spot.
- Every crow is 20% faster during a blood moon.

| Night | Black (1 HP) | Red (2) | Purple (3) | Gold (5) | White (8) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blood moon 1 | 4 | 8 | 7 | 5 | 0 | 24 |

## 2. A buff after each blood moon you survive (Caleb: pick one)

At dawn, before the shop, a window asks: **CHOOSE A BLESSING**
- ATTACK UP: +0.5 damage on every weapon hit
- SCARECROWS UP: +0.5 damage on every scarecrow shot
- +1 HEART: one more heart (up to 5)

A lost practice night gives no blessing. Buffs last the rest of the run.

## 3. Weapons (WEAPONS page)

You hold one weapon. Buying a new one replaces the one in your hand.
Each weapon has its own upgrades: once you have it, its BUY line becomes its upgrade line
("BUY SPEAR = $120" -> "SPEAR LV2 = $90" -> "SPEAR LV3 = $180" -> "SPEAR LV3 MAX"). Caleb: yes.

| Weapon | Buy | Lv.1 / Lv.2 / Lv.3 damage | Upgrades (Lv.2 / Lv.3) | How it hits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wooden sword | start | 1 / 1.5 / 2 | 40 / 80 | in front, quick (today's sword; reach grows at Lv.3) |
| Iron sword | 100 | 3 / 3.5 / 4 | 70 / 140 | in front, like the wooden sword; slower swing |
| Spear | 80 | 2 / 2.5 / 3 | 60 / 120 | a long jab, 2 tiles straight ahead; narrow |
| Scythe | 110 | 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 | 80 / 160 | a wide sweep: the tile in front and the two beside it; slow |
| Slingshot | 120 | 2 / 2.5 / 3 | 80 / 160 | a stone straight ahead, any distance; one target, reload |

The scythe is a weapon the farmer holds, separate from the sickle (the farming tool). Caleb, 2026-10-22.
**Switching back** to a weapon you had before brings it back at the level it had (Caleb).

The hammer is out (Caleb).

## 5. The crow cannon (Caleb: yes, and stock it with pumpkins)

- Buy it on the FARM page: 160 coins, max 2. It appears on a free fence corner, so it takes no crop slot
  and needs no placing.
- **Ammo is pumpkins.** Stand next to a cannon and press A: the pumpkins in your bag go into it, up to 10 per
  cannon. Each shot uses 1. A loaded pumpkin can no longer be sold (20 coins), so it is a real choice.
  With a cannon, SELL ALL keeps pumpkins in the bag; the SELL page's PUMPKIN line sells them on purpose.
- Every 3 s a loaded cannon lobs a pumpkin at the nearest crow within 120 px. It bursts where it lands and
  does 3 damage to every crow within one tile.
- Cannons keep their pumpkins from night to night.
